# Creative-direction review

## What weakened the original presentation
- **Home:** orbit rings, a floating prism and achievement chips competed with the portrait; every section used the same spacious heading-and-panel rhythm. Journey and learning grids repeated the same rounded-container language.
- **Shared headings:** oversized geometric headings, gold italic endings and widely spaced labels appeared everywhere with little variation. Aggressive negative tracking weakened readability.
- **About:** academic achievements looked like dashboard tiles; biography, values and timeline lacked a distinct editorial structure.
- **Expertise:** dozens of identical expandable cards buried the information hierarchy and made practical skills look equivalent to exploratory topics.
- **Projects:** a large card wrapped another framed preview; the rest repeated identical thumbnails and boxed summaries without a portfolio-like viewing rhythm.
- **Project details:** metadata and individual features were unnecessarily boxed, interrupting the case study.
- **Experience:** nested cards made the current apprenticeship resemble a feature grid, rather than a professional narrative.
- **Learning:** stage cards, topic cards and draft cards all used the same shapes, despite serving different purposes.
- **Contact:** the form and contact methods were equally weighted boxes; the primary action lacked a clear working-surface hierarchy.
- **Site shell:** pill-heavy navigation, ambient blobs, decorative particles and the repeated rounded contact invitation made the pages feel interchangeable.

## Redesign direction
Keep the existing identity, portrait, factual content and functionality. Replace ornamental effects with photographic presence, an editorial scale, asymmetrical project spreads, ledger-like skills and education, unframed timeline details, and a purposefully frosted contact desk. Use gold sparingly for selection, rules and emphasis. Interactions reinforce the information: expanding rows, animated filter states, subtle portrait movement and restrained arrow movement. Preserve keyboard operation and reduced-motion support.
